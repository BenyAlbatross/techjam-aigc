(PWCL), yielding a feature vector, Xcls , that is rich in fine-grained forensic details. Concurrently, the Prompt Flow Module learns a
probabilistic distribution from its conditioning inputs and generates N prompt samples from it via Monte Carlo sampling. These samples
are fused with a repository of B base prompt sets, yielding B × N text embeddings. The final prediction is determined by averaging the
cosine similarities between the visual token (Xcls ) and the resulting text embeddings.

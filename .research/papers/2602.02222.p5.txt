patch-level features from real images to encode Reality Priors within an orthogonal memory bank that approximates the real-image
manifold. In Phase 2, the learned priors are frozen. Given an input image, MIRROR constructs a manifold-consistent Ideal
Reference through sparse projection and identifies forgeries via Reference-Comparison. This process utilizes both the reconstruct
perplexity and the comparison residual to evaluate whether the input deviates from the learned reality manifold.

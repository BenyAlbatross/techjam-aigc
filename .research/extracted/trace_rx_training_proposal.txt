 04 / SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE


 TRACE-RX: evidence experts with reliability-aware fusion
 TRACE-RX combines one selected global representation backbone with a native-resolution forensic expert, authentic-manifold evidence and controlled intervention responses.
 JPEG, resize and blur in the diagram are illustrative intervention operators; the final intervention set is selected empirically.




 Core evidence score: effective evidence = signed expert evidence x availability / uncertainty. Intervention responses are fusion features, not independent votes. A constrained covariance layer reduces
 double-counting when experts react to the same artifact.

 05 / NATIVE FORENSIC EXPERT


 Preserve local evidence that global resizing can destroy
 A single 224-pixel global resize can erase the exact microtexture, sampling and residual patterns that image forensics needs. The forensic expert therefore analyses

 07 / CONTROLLED INTERVENTIONS


 Estimate whether forensic evidence remains observable
 A controlled intervention is a mild, deterministic transformation applied temporarily at inference. It is a stress test, not a new training label and not a vote. The model compares an
 expert's representation before and after intervention to estimate local sensitivity at the observed endpoint.

 Response representation
 For expert feature h and logit z, define r_p(x)=[|z(x)-z(T_p(x))|, 1-cos(h(x),h(T_p(x))), ||h(x)-h(T_p(x))||_2/(||h(x)||_2+epsilon)]. A small response is interpreted jointly with original
 evidence strength and estimated image quality: it may indicate stable evidence or an already-saturated image.
 Intervention-set utility: U(P)=Delta TPR@1%FPR + alpha Delta worst-group AUC - lambda latency(P) - mu instability(P). Greedy forward selection stops when marginal held-out utility becomes negligible.

 14 / ERROR ANALYSIS


 Turn failures into explanations and the next data decision
 Error analysis operates on master lineages, not individual transformed records. Every false positive, false negative and abstention is joined to source, generator, authentic
 subtype, content stratum and complete transformation history.
